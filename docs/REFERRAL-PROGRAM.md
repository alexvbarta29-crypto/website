# Customer referral program

A "Give $25, Get $50" referral system that lives on the website at
**bartawindowwashing.com/referral**, reached from one small "Referral
Program" link in the footer and the link the office texts to customers. It
runs on the same Netlify stack as the quote form (Netlify Functions +
Rotor CRM), with Netlify Blobs as its own small database. No third-party
referral SaaS, no accounts or passwords for customers.

## The offer (single source of truth)

| Who                       | What they get                                        |
|---------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| The friend being referred | **$25 off** their first service                      |
| The customer who referred | **$50 account credit** *or* a **$25 gift card**, per friend who books. They pick which once the friend's first job is complete. |

Amounts live in exactly two places that must be kept in sync:

- `build/sitedata.py` → `REFERRAL` (drives every number printed on the pages)
- `netlify/lib/referral-config.mjs` → `REWARDS` (drives API responses, SMS
  text, and CRM notes)

## How it works, end to end

1. **The owner texts a customer** `https://www.bartawindowwashing.com/referral`
   ("Want $50 off? Refer a friend."). The same page sits behind the footer
   link, and nowhere else on the site.
2. **The customer fills in the form**: their own name, phone, optional
   email, and who they're referring — **first name, last name and mobile
   number, nothing more**, all three required (up to 10 friends at once).
   All the office does with a referred friend is text them a link, and
   their own form on `/r/CODE` collects the address, email and job details,
   so asking the referrer for any of it twice would only slow them down.
   They confirm they have their friends' permission to share their details.
3. **The site stores the referral** (Netlify Blobs), **creates a lead in
   Rotor** for each friend (tag `Referral`, source `Referral program`, and
   notes that are the exact text to send them — see below), and, when SMS
   is on, **texts the friend, the
   referrer, and/or the office** (see `REFERRAL_SMS_MODE`).
   The customer sees a success screen with their share code, a copy-able
   share link, one-tap "Text a friend" / "Email" buttons, and a private
   tracking link.
4. **The office reaches out to the friend** ("Hi Jane, Alex referred you,
   here's $25 off"), automatically via Twilio or by hand. Both by-hand
   routes write the message for you: the friend's Rotor lead notes *are*
   the message (copy the box, send it), and the admin dashboard's "Text
   friend" link opens the phone's messaging app with the same text already
   filled in.
5. **The friend books.** The short link `/r/CODE` lands them on the
   **ordinary quote form** (`/get-quote.html`) with their code already in
   the promo field and a band across the top reading "Alex referred you!
   Your first service is $25 off, and we've already applied it below." One
   form, one funnel, and the lead arrives in the shape the office already
   works. They can also come through the quote form or a phone call on
   their own. A quote request that carries the
   code as its promo code, or comes from a referred phone number, is tagged
   `Referral` in Rotor and moves the referral to *Quote requested* by
   itself. Friends who were never listed but got the link from the customer
   are recorded under that customer too, so they earn the reward.
6. **The job gets done and paid.** The office marks the referral *Booked*
   when the friend books, then *Job complete* when the first job is finished
   and paid. That unlocks the reward: the referrer gets a text with a link
   to their tracking page, where they pick the **$50 credit or the $25 gift
   card** (automatic in SMS mode `all`; otherwise the dashboard's "Text
   referrer" link carries the same message).
7. **The office issues the reward** from the dashboard (it defaults to what
   the referrer picked), which marks the referral *Reward issued*. The
   tracking page updates immediately, and "Text referrer" now offers a
   "your reward has been issued" text.

## Pages

| URL | Purpose | Indexed |
|-----|---------|---------|
| `/referral` (serves `referral.html`) | Program page: offer, how it works, the referral form, program terms. With `?t=TOKEN` it also shows the referrer's private dashboard (their referrals, statuses, the reward pick, share tools) and lets them add more friends. | no (footer link + texted link only) |
| `/r/CODE` (serves `referred.html`) | Friend landing: "Alex sent you $25 off", the claim form, what Barta does, call button. Unknown code → generic version. Generated with root-absolute URLs so it works under `/r/`. | no |
| `/admin/referrals.html` | Office dashboard, protected by `REFERRAL_ADMIN_KEY`. No analytics. | no (`noindex` + `robots.txt` Disallow) |

All pages are generated by `build/build.py` like the rest of the site
(`python3 build/build.py`):

- `build/referral_pages.py` → `referral_page(depth)` and `referred_page(depth)`
- `build/referral_admin_page.py` → `referral_admin_page(depth)`
- Page-specific styling/behaviour: `assets/css/referral.css`,
  `assets/js/referral.js`, `assets/css/referral-admin.css`,
  `assets/js/referral-admin.js` (minified to `*.min.*` by the build and
  cache-busted with `?v=` like `styles.css` / `main.js`)
- `_redirects` gains the two rewrites (`/referral`, `/r/:code`);
  `robots.txt` disallows `/admin/`.

### `/r/CODE` — the referred friend's link

`/r/:code` is a **200 rewrite to `/get-quote.html?promo=:code`**, so the
short link stays in the address bar. Two consequences worth knowing:

- The query string never reaches the browser, so `main.js` reads the code
  back out of the path (`/r/([A-Za-z0-9-]+)`) as well as from `?promo=` /
  `?ref=`. It fills `#q-promo` and calls `GET /api/referral?code=` for the
  referrer's first name, then reveals the `[data-referral-banner]` band.
  An unrecognised code leaves the field filled (so the office still sees
  what they typed) but shows **no** banner — never promise a discount that
  can't be honoured. The banner's own `display` must stay paired with a
  `.quote-referral[hidden] { display: none }` rule: an author `display`
  beats the browser's `[hidden]`, and the band would show unconditionally.
- `get-quote.html` is written through `_root_absolute()` for the same
  reason `referred.html` is: served at `/r/CODE`, relative URLs would
  resolve under `/r/` and the page would arrive with no CSS and no JS.

From there `/api/lead` does the rest — `referralForLead()` recognises the
promo code (or the friend's phone number) and moves the referral to
`quoted` with the `Referral` tag on the Rotor lead.

`referred.html` and `/api/claim` — the older standalone landing page with
its own short claim form — are **still built and still work**, they are
just no longer what `/r/CODE` serves. Switching back is one line in
`build_redirects()`.

## Environment variables (Netlify → Site configuration → Environment variables)

| Variable | Required | Purpose |
|----------|----------|---------|
| `ROTOR_API_KEY` | yes (already set for the quote form) | Creates referred friends and claims as leads in Rotor. |
| `REFERRAL_ADMIN_KEY` | yes, for the dashboard | Long random secret (32+ chars, e.g. `openssl rand -base64 32`) the office enters on `/admin/referrals.html`. Without it the admin API returns 503 and the dashboard says so. |
| `REFERRAL_SMS_MODE` | no (default `off`) | `off` = no automatic texts (office texts by hand from the dashboard). `office` = text the office number on every new referral. `all` = also text the friend (welcome), the referrer (tracking link), and the referrer again when a job is marked complete (pick your reward). |
| `TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID`, `TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN`, `TWILIO_FROM` | only when SMS mode ≠ `off` | Twilio credentials; `TWILIO_FROM` is the sending number in E.164 (`+17633143400`) or a Messaging Service SID (`MG…`). |
| `REFERRAL_OFFICE_PHONE` | only for SMS modes `office`/`all` | Where new-referral alerts are texted (E.164). |
| `SITE_URL` | no | Base for links in texts and API responses. Defaults to Netlify's own `URL` for the deploy, so production, branch deploys and deploy previews each link to themselves. |

Netlify Blobs needs no configuration; it is available to every Netlify
Function automatically and the data survives deploys. Nothing referral-
related works on the GitHub Pages preview (no functions), where the forms
show the honest call-us fallback.

> **Texting referred friends.** Mode `all` sends a text to a person who has
> not themselves opted in, on the strength of their friend's confirmation
> that they have permission. Keep the message to a single informational
> text with an opt-out line (the templates below do), or leave the mode at
> `off`/`office` and text from the dashboard by hand. Have this reviewed
> against TCPA/CTIA guidance before turning `all` on.

## Testing it before it goes live

Everything runs on a **Netlify branch deploy or deploy preview** exactly as
it will in production, with its own copy of the data, so the whole flow can
be walked with a real phone before the change reaches the live site:

1. In Netlify, either open a pull request for the branch (Netlify posts a
   `deploy-preview-N--<site>.netlify.app` link on it) or add the branch under
   **Site configuration → Build & deploy → Branches and deploy contexts**
   (giving `<branch>--<site>.netlify.app`).
2. Make sure `REFERRAL_ADMIN_KEY` (and `ROTOR_API_KEY`) apply to that
   context: Netlify env vars apply to all contexts unless scoped.
3. Walk the checklist below on the preview address with your own phone as
   the friend. Test referrals create real leads in Rotor tagged `Referral`;
   delete them there afterwards, and delete the test referrals from the
   dashboard. Texts are only sent if Twilio is configured for that context.
4. When it behaves, merge to the production branch; the same code deploys
   with the live data store.

Locally, `npm test` runs 130+ unit tests against an in-memory store with
the CRM and Twilio stubbed, and `python3 build/build.py` regenerates the
pages.

## Data model (Netlify Blobs, store `referrals`)

All records are JSON. Timestamps are ISO-8601 UTC strings. Phone numbers
are stored both as typed (`phone`) and normalized to 10 digits
(`phone_digits`, US only, leading `1` stripped); the digits are the
identity everywhere.

```
referrer/{referrer_id}         referrer_id = phone_digits
  { id, code, token, first_name, last_name, phone, phone_digits, email,
    reward_pref: null | "credit" | "giftcard"   (their latest pick),
    consent_at, source_page, created_at, updated_at }

code/{CODE}                    -> { referrer_id }        public share code, e.g. BARTA-7K3XQ
token/{TOKEN}                  -> { referrer_id }        private tracking token, 24 chars, base32

referral/{referral_id}         referral_id = "r_" + base36 time + 6 random chars
  { id, referrer_id, code, referrer_name, referrer_phone,
    first_name, last_name, phone, phone_digits, email, address, note,
    office_note,
    status: "new" | "contacted" | "quoted" | "booked" | "completed" | "rewarded" | "declined",
    history: [ { status, at, by: "system" | "office" | "lead-form" | "customer", note } ],
    reward: null | { type: null | "credit" | "giftcard", amount, chosen_at, issued_at, note },
    reward_ready_at: null | ISO        (set when marked completed),
    duplicate_of: null | referral_id,
    rotor: { delivered: bool, status: number | null, at },
    sms: { friend: bool, referrer: bool, office: bool, reward_ready?: bool },
    quote_requested_at: null | ISO,
    created_at, updated_at }

idx/referrer/{referrer_id}/{referral_id}  -> { id }     lists a referrer's referrals
idx/phone/{friend_phone_digits}            -> { id }     first referral of this friend (dedupe + claim/quote matching)
```

Codes use the alphabet `ABCDEFGHJKLMNPQRSTUVWXYZ23456789` (no 0/O/1/I),
five characters after the `BARTA-` prefix. Lookups are case-insensitive.

### Statuses

| Status | Meaning | Set by |
|--------|---------|--------|
| `new` | Referral received; office has not reached out yet. | system |
| `contacted` | Office has texted/called the friend. | office |
| `quoted` | The friend claimed the offer / requested a quote. | claim form or quote form (automatic), or office |
| `booked` | The friend booked their first service. | office |
| `completed` | First job finished and paid: reward owed. `reward.type` is `null` until the referrer picks. | office (texts the referrer in SMS mode `all`) |
| `rewarded` | Reward issued to the referrer (`reward` filled in). | office (`issue_reward`) |
| `declined` | Not eligible / not interested / duplicate. | office |

The office can move a referral to any status at any time; `rewarded` is
set through `issue_reward` so the reward details are always recorded.
Leaving `completed` for an earlier stage, or `rewarded` for any other
stage, clears the reward (and the pick).

### Duplicates

A friend phone number that already has a referral (from anyone) is
accepted but flagged: the new record gets `duplicate_of` set to the first
referral's id and status `new`; it is **not** sent to Rotor again and no
friend text goes out. The referrer sees it as a normal "Sent" entry; the
office sees a "Duplicate" badge and decides. A referrer can never refer
their own phone number.

## API (Netlify Functions, same origin)

All responses are JSON with `Cache-Control: no-store`. Errors are
`{ "ok": false, "error": "<human readable>", "field": "<optional dotted path>" }`.
Every read endpoint answers `503` when Netlify Blobs itself is unavailable.

### `POST /api/referral` — submit referrals (public)

```json
{
  "referrer": { "first_name": "Alex", "last_name": "Barta", "phone": "(763) 555-0100",
                "email": "alex@example.com" },
  "friends": [
    { "first_name": "Jane", "last_name": "Doe", "phone": "763-555-0101" }
  ],
  "consent": true,
  "page": "/referral",
  "token": "optional — when adding friends from an existing tracking page"
}
```

Rules: referrer first name + valid 10-digit US phone required; `consent`
must be `true`; 1–10 friends; each friend needs a first name, a last name
and a valid phone; a friend's phone can't equal the referrer's; duplicates within one
submission collapse to one. `referrer.reward_pref` (`credit`/`giftcard`) is
optional. Field caps: names 60, phone 25, email 120, address 200, note 500
chars; longer values are rejected with `400` and the field path.

The referral form sends only `first_name`, `last_name` and `phone` per
friend. A friend's `email`, `address` and `note` are still **accepted and
stored** when present, because `recordClaim()`
(`netlify/lib/referral-claim.mjs`) fills them in for a friend who arrives
through the share link and asks for a quote — that friend typed them on
their own form. Don't remove them from the record shape.

Success `200`:

```json
{
  "ok": true,
  "code": "BARTA-7K3XQ",
  "token": "K7Q2M9X4P8W3N6R5T2Y7V4B9",
  "share_url": "https://www.bartawindowwashing.com/r/BARTA-7K3XQ",
  "status_url": "https://www.bartawindowwashing.com/referral?t=K7Q2M9X4P8W3N6R5T2Y7V4B9",
  "returning": false,
  "referrer": { "first_name": "Alex", "reward_pref": null },
  "friends": [ { "id": "r_m1abc2xyz123", "first_name": "Jane", "status": "new", "duplicate": false } ],
  "stored": true,
  "delivered": true
}
```

`stored: false` means Netlify Blobs was unavailable (referrals still went to
Rotor; the tracking link will not work). `delivered: false` means Rotor
rejected/was unreachable for at least one friend (they are still stored and
show in the dashboard). Only when **both** fail does the request return
`502` so the page shows the call-us fallback. An existing referrer (same
phone) keeps their existing code; `token` and `status_url` are only
returned for a **new** referrer or when the request carries the referrer's
existing `token` (they came from their tracking link). Otherwise both are
`null` and `returning: true`: the referrals are still accepted, but a phone
number alone never unlocks someone's private link or changes their details.
The customer's original link keeps working, and the office can resend it
from the dashboard.

#### The Rotor lead each friend gets

Source `Referral program`, tag `Referral`, no `service_type` (they haven't
asked for anything yet), and **notes that are the text to send them and
nothing else** — `rotorLeadPayload()` in `netlify/lib/referral-notify.mjs`:

```
Hi Jane! Alex B. referred you to Barta Window Washing, so your first service is $25 off. Claim it here: https://www.bartawindowwashing.com/r/BARTA-7K3XQ or call (763) 314-3400. Reply with any questions.
```

No heading, no preamble, nothing to hand-select around: open the lead,
select the notes box (or use Rotor's share button on it) and send. The text
is `SMS.friend()` from `netlify/lib/referral-config.mjs` word for word, so a
hand-sent message and an automatic one (SMS mode `all`) read identically,
and the link is the friend's own page — `/r/CODE` greets them by the
referrer's name and shows the "Claim my $25 off" form.

Everything the notes deliberately leave out — the referrer's phone, the
$50/$25 reward breakdown, the note the referrer wrote about this friend, the
status — is on the referral dashboard, which is where that context is
worked. The message itself still names the referrer ("Alex B. referred
you") and carries the code inside its link, so a Rotor lead on its own is
never anonymous. `/api/claim` and `/api/lead` leads are different: those
friends have already asked for a quote, so their notes stay descriptive.

### `POST /api/referral` with `{ "action": "choose_reward" }` — the referrer picks

```json
{ "action": "choose_reward", "t": "K7Q2M9X4P8W3N6R5T2Y7V4B9", "id": "r_…", "reward_type": "giftcard" }
```

Allowed only from the tracking page (the token must own the referral), only
while the referral is `completed`, and until the office issues it (`409`
otherwise). The pick can be changed until then. It sets
`reward.type/amount/chosen_at`, adds a history entry `by: "customer"`, and
becomes the referrer's `reward_pref`. Returns `{ "ok": true, "referral": {…dashboard entry…} }`.

### `GET /api/referral?t=TOKEN` — private tracking page (referrer)

```json
{
  "ok": true,
  "referrer": { "first_name": "Alex", "last_name": "Barta", "phone": "(763) 555-0100",
                "email": "alex@example.com", "reward_pref": "giftcard", "code": "BARTA-7K3XQ" },
  "share_url": "https://www.bartawindowwashing.com/r/BARTA-7K3XQ",
  "totals": { "referred": 3, "booked": 2, "rewarded": 1, "pending": 1, "to_choose": 1,
              "credit_earned": 50, "gift_cards_earned": 0 },
  "referrals": [
    { "id": "r_…", "friend_name": "Jane D.", "status": "completed",
      "created_at": "…", "updated_at": "…",
      "reward": { "type": null, "amount": null, "chosen_at": null, "issued_at": null } }
  ]
}
```

`totals.pending` counts `new`/`contacted`/`quoted`; `booked` counts
`booked` + `completed` + `rewarded`; `to_choose` counts completed jobs
whose reward hasn't been picked. Unknown token → `404`.

### `GET /api/referral?code=CODE` — public share-code lookup (friend page)

```json
{ "ok": true, "code": "BARTA-7K3XQ", "referrer_first_name": "Alex",
  "friend_discount": 25, "referrer_credit": 50, "referrer_gift_card": 25 }
```

Deliberately exposes only the referrer's first name. Unknown code → `404`.

### `POST /api/claim` — a referred friend claims the offer (public)

```json
{ "code": "BARTA-7K3XQ", "first_name": "Jane", "last_name": "Doe", "phone": "763-555-0101",
  "email": "", "address": "123 Main St, Delano", "services": ["Gutter Cleaning"],
  "note": "Best after 5pm", "consent": true, "page": "/r/BARTA-7K3XQ" }
```

First name, a valid phone and `consent: true` are required; `code` is
optional (a mistyped code is kept in the notes for the office). The claim
always becomes a Rotor lead (source `Referral program`, tag `Referral`,
`service_type` from the services, notes with the referral code and referrer,
the services and the friend's note). Then, by code and/or phone: a listed
friend's referral moves `new`/`contacted` → `quoted`; a friend nobody listed
gets a `quoted` record created under the referrer; a customer using their
own code is not treated as a referral (tag `Referral program`, note "not a
referral"). Response
`{ "ok": true, "code": "BARTA-7K3XQ", "referrer_first_name": "Alex", "delivered": true, "stored": true }`;
`code` is `null` when nobody referred them; `502` only when the CRM failed
**and** nothing could be recorded.

### Quote form integration (`/api/lead`)

`netlify/functions/lead.mjs` runs one best-effort step (`netlify/lib/referral-hook.mjs`,
behind a 1.5 s timeout, so it can never break or delay a quote request): if
`promo_code` is a referral share code that exists, or the lead's phone
matches a referred friend, the Rotor payload gets the tag `Referral` and a
notes line `Referral code: BARTA-7K3XQ (referred by Alex Barta)`, and the
referral moves to `quoted` (created on the spot for a share-link friend,
with a history note "Requested a quote on the website").

### Admin — `/api/referral/admin`

Every request carries `Authorization: Bearer <REFERRAL_ADMIN_KEY>`.
Wrong/missing key → `401`; key not configured on the server → `503`.

`GET /api/referral/admin` (optional `?status=new&q=jane`):

```json
{
  "ok": true,
  "stats": { "total": 12, "by_status": { "new": 3, "contacted": 2, "quoted": 1, "booked": 2, "completed": 1, "rewarded": 2, "declined": 1 },
             "rewards_owed": 1, "credit_issued": 100, "gift_cards_issued": 1 },
  "referrals": [ { …full referral record… } ],
  "referrers": [ { "id": "7635550100", "code": "BARTA-7K3XQ", "first_name": "Alex", "last_name": "Barta",
                   "phone": "(763) 555-0100", "email": "…", "reward_pref": "giftcard", "created_at": "…",
                   "status_url": "https://www.bartawindowwashing.com/referral?t=…",
                   "referred": 3, "booked": 2, "rewarded": 1 } ]
}
```

Newest referrals first. `rewards_owed` counts `completed` referrals.
`?status=` and `?q=` narrow only `referrals`; `stats` stay global and
`referrers` is always the full list (each with a ready-made `status_url` so
the office can resend a customer's tracking link; the raw token is never
returned). `GET /api/referral/admin?format=csv` returns `text/csv` (UTF-8
with a byte-order mark, so Excel opens it correctly) of every referral for a
spreadsheet; cells that start with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` are neutralized.

`POST /api/referral/admin` with one of:

```json
{ "action": "set_status",  "id": "r_…", "status": "completed", "note": "Paid 9/12" }
{ "action": "issue_reward", "id": "r_…", "reward_type": "giftcard", "note": "Mailed 9/14" }
{ "action": "set_note",     "id": "r_…", "note": "Office-only note" }
{ "action": "set_reward_pref", "referrer_id": "7635550100", "reward_pref": "giftcard" }
{ "action": "delete",       "id": "r_…" }
```

Each returns `{ "ok": true, "referral": {…} }` (or `{ "ok": true, "referrer": {…} }`,
or `{ "ok": true, "deleted": "r_…" }`). `set_status` to `completed` stamps
`reward_ready_at`, opens the pick, and in SMS mode `all` texts the referrer.
`issue_reward` sets `status: "rewarded"` and `reward: { type, amount,
chosen_at, issued_at, note }` (`reward_type` defaults to the referrer's pick,
then their preference on file). Every status change appends to `history`
with `by: "office"`. Guard rails: `issue_reward` on a referral already
`rewarded` answers `409`; `set_status` to `rewarded` answers `400`; ids must
look like `r_…` and `referrer_id` must be a 10-digit phone (`404` otherwise).

## Text message templates

Used by the Twilio sender and, verbatim, by the dashboard's one-tap `sms:`
links (so manual and automatic texts read the same).

- **Friend (new referral):** `Hi {friend_first}! {referrer_first} {referrer_last_initial}. referred you to Barta Window Washing, so your first service is $25 off. Claim it here: {share_url} or call (763) 314-3400. Reply with any questions.`
- **Referrer (confirmation):** `Thanks for referring {n} friend(s) to Barta Window Washing! You earn a $50 credit (or a $25 gift card) for each one who books. Track your referrals: {status_url}`
- **Referrer (job complete, pick your reward):** `Great news from Barta Window Washing: {friend_first}'s first service is complete, so your reward is ready! Pick a $50 account credit or a $25 gift card here: {status_url}`
- **Referrer (reward issued, manual from the dashboard):** `Great news from Barta Window Washing: your {reward} for referring {friend_first} has been issued. Thanks for spreading the word!`
- **Office alert:** `New referral: {referrer_name} ({referrer_phone}) referred {friend_name} ({friend_phone}). Code {code}. {site}/admin/referrals.html`

When the referrer gave no last name the friend text reads `Alex referred
you…`. The customer's own "Text a friend" / "Email a friend" buttons send a
shorter personal note from their own phone.

## Admin dashboard (`/admin/referrals.html`)

Phone-friendly, app-like page. Enter the admin key once (kept in
`sessionStorage`). Shows: stat tiles (New, Contacted, Quote requested,
Booked, Job complete · reward owed, Rewards issued, credit and gift cards
issued), status filter tabs, a search box (name / phone / code), and a card
per referral with friend and referrer details, created date, status, a
status selector, "Text friend" / "Call friend" / "Text referrer" one-tap
links (the referrer text changes with the stage: tracking link → pick your
