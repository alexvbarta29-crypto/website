    Program terms

    The fine print, in plain English

    Who counts as a new customer?
    Anyone whose household hasn’t had a service from Barta Window Washing before. If we’ve already cleaned for them, or for someone else at their address, they’re already family, so a referral doesn’t apply.
    When do I get my reward?
    As soon as your friend’s first service is complete and paid. We mark it done, and you get a text with a link to pick your $50 credit or $25 gift card. Your private tracking link shows every step along the way.
    How is the credit or gift card delivered?
    Account credit is applied automatically and comes off your next invoice with us, there’s nothing to remember. A gift card is texted or mailed to you within a few days of your pick. You choose each time, so you can take credit for one friend and a gift card for the next.
    Is there a limit to how many friends I can refer?
    No. Refer as many friends as you like, up to 10 in one go, and earn a reward for every one who books. It’s one reward per referred household, and the friend’s $25 applies to their first service only.
    Can the program change?
    We may adjust or end the program at any time, but every referral you’ve already sent is honored under the terms shown when you sent it.
    I have a question about one of my referrals.
    Call or text us at (763) 314-3400 or email office@bartawindowwashing.com. We’re happy to help.
