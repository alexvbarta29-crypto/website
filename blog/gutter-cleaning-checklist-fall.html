The Minnesota Fall Gutter-Cleaning Checklist

Before the first freeze, run through these steps to protect your foundation, fascia, and roof through a Minnesota winter, and what to check first.


Technician clearing a gutter by hand from a ladder

Why fall is critical

Clogged gutters in winter mean ice dams, overflow, and water pooling against your foundation. Clearing them before the first freeze is one of the cheapest, highest-impact things you can do to protect your home.

The checklist

Clear all gutters of leaves and debris by hand. Flush downspouts and confirm water flows freely to the ground and away from the house. Check for sagging sections and loose hangers. Inspect seams for leaks. Look at the roof edge for damage or missing shingles.

Don't forget the extensions

Make sure downspout extensions carry water at least four to six feet away from your foundation. Water pooling at the base of your home is a leading cause of basement leaks and foundation issues.
